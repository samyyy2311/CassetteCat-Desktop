# Code of Conduct

CassetteCat Desktop is a respectful space for users and contributors.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and opinions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be handled fairly and discreetly.
